AGENDA ITEM 07

Special Recognition: The Canadian OGs

On September 17, the gang had zero members in Canada. Two meters is line of sight, the nearest members were hundreds of miles away on the wrong side of the border, and the rules say you only get in by making a 2-meter CW contact with someone already in the gang. Two Canadians looked at that problem and decided to overcome it in the most difficult ways available. The Board recognizes them both.

Case Study A · Alberta

Vince, VE6LK, BKG #500

Vince did not want an easy induction at a picnic table or on a summit with everyone standing around. His rule was that each station had to be in its own country. So he drove more than three hours south from home to the Alberta–Montana border. Devin "Gato" Felix, N7HNT, drove about four hours north from Helena, Montana, to meet him on the other side.

Driving
Roughly seven hours, combined, one way
Distance
About 2.5 kilometers, station to station
Power
5 watts or less, the whole time
Result
First Canadian member, September 18, 2026

Vince's write-up asks whether a contact across the border counts as DX. His ruling was yes, even at 2.5 kilometers. The Board agrees.

Return on investment: In the 11 days after he joined, Vince recruited 15 new members, including 13 in Alberta. Seven hours of driving helped deliver the gang's second-biggest day ever, September 26, with 31 new members.

Case Study B · Ontario

Josh, VE3YTX, BKG #531

Josh is a new ham who caught the CW bug. When he started, there were no members in Canada, and the closest ones were about 200 miles away in the United States. On 2 meters, that is a rough situation. A member suggested he build his own Yagi antenna and try for a 2-meter CW contact through a satellite instead.

Method
2-meter CW, by satellite, through space
Antenna
A homebrew Yagi he built for the job
Contact
N9HO, the gang's Alabama and Satellite OG
Attempts
One. It worked on the first try.

His write-up is titled "A New Ham's Guide to Overcomplicating Everything." The Board would like to state that this is the correct amount of complicated.

Return on investment: Ontario OG, joined September 22, 2026. He recruited 3 more Ontario members within 3 days.

Combined impact: Canada went from 0 members to 19 in 12 days. One man drove to a border. One man went to space. Both of these are now considered official BKG recruiting strategies.

AGENDA ITEM 08

Audit Findings

In the interest of transparency and good corporate governance, our internal audit team (one guy with a spreadsheet) reviewed every row. The following irregularities were found.

Finding 1

Two members are recorded as joining in the year 26 A.D. One join date reads "8/20/0026." Another reads "0026-09-03."

Resolution: Corrected to 2026. Ham radio was not invented yet, so the auditors accepted the correction.
Finding 2

88 of the 328 new members, or 27%, had their names entered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS.

Resolution: None required. Excitement is a leading indicator.
Finding 3

Five new members are listed by first name only, including Don, Steve, Geoff, Ethan, and Brent. Like Cher.

Resolution: Their callsigns are on file. That is all the government needs, and it is all we need.
Finding 4

On Saturday, September 5, the gang recruited zero members. This is the only day in the review period with no growth.

Resolution: A meeting has been scheduled about the meeting. Then we recruited 14 people the next Saturday.
Finding 5

Membership numbers were not always issued in date order. BKG #283 is dated August 19, the day before BKG #276.

Resolution: Hamfest paperwork was processed in the order it came out of people's pockets. This is fine.
