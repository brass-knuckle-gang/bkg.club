WHAT CHANGES

  • The roster gets its own page. index.html keeps the Godfather, the newest five, the headcount and a big button to the full roster. The home page stops carrying 700 cards.
  • List is the default on phones. One 60px row per member instead of a 300px card, so about 5× less scrolling. The mugshot wall is still there, three across on a phone.
  • Search and filters. Callsign, name, BKG # and state all search. The filters cover new members, state OGs, recruiters and members with photos.
  • Blocks of 100 with a jump bar. The sticky header always shows which hundred you are in, and one tap jumps to any block.
  • Rap sheet per member. Shows joined date, territory, who recruited them and who they recruited, a QRZ link, and a shareable link (bkg.club/roster#KI7QCF).
  • Badges become chips. The diagonal ribbons, card tilt, hover zoom and flashing NEW!! go away. On a phone they overlapped and couldn't be read.
  • No-JS safe. The build still writes every member into the HTML, so site_contract.py keeps checking it. Search, sorting and the sheet are layered on top.
